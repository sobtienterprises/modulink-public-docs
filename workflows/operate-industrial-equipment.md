# Operate Industrial Equipment

Use this workflow for Indi Wi-Fi or Indi LoRa after commissioning is complete.

## 1. Check terminal status

Open Dashboard or Devices on the Basestation. Check that the terminal is online.
Check the time of the most recent report. Do not act on a terminal with stale or
unknown status.

## 2. Review readings

Open the terminal in Instrument Manager. Review the live readings and history.
Compare values with the expected operating range for the connected instrument.

## 3. Review alerts

Open Alerts. Read the alert condition and its time. Correct the field condition
before you clear or acknowledge an alert.

## 4. Use an output

Use an output only when the connected equipment is safe to operate. Send one
command. Wait for the terminal report. Confirm the result at the equipment.

## 5. Record a problem

If readings or output state are incorrect, record the product name, terminal
identity, time, and observed condition. Contact Modulink support if the condition
continues.
