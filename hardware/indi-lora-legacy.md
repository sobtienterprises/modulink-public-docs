# Indi LoRa (Legacy)

Indi LoRa (Legacy) is for existing installations only. It is a LoRaWAN terminal
with two 4–20 mA process-input channels. Do not use this page to select or
specify new Indi LoRa equipment.

## Use this page when

- You maintain an installed Indi LoRa (Legacy) terminal.
- The terminal label or your installation record identifies it as legacy.
- You need to confirm its normal reporting and configuration path.

## Installation rule

Keep the existing hardware profile and field wiring. Do not replace the profile
with an Indi LoRa or Agri profile. Product profiles are not interchangeable.

## Next step

Follow [Connect Indi LoRa or Agri](../workflows/connect-lora-terminal.md). If
you plan to replace a legacy terminal, contact Modulink before you change field
wiring or output configuration.
