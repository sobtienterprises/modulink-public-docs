# Modulink Basestation

The Modulink Basestation is the on-site hub for your Modulink system. It runs
the software that receives terminal data and provides dashboards, alerts,
history, and user access.

## What it connects

- Indi Wi-Fi terminals through your site network.
- Indi LoRa and Agri terminals through the on-site LoRaWAN network.
- Your database or business application through the API, when included in your
  deployment scope.

## Before installation

- Choose a dry location with stable power.
- Confirm access to your site network.
- Confirm LoRa coverage if you will use Indi LoRa or Agri.
- Identify the people who will manage users, backups, and recovery.

## What you do after setup

Use a browser on the site network to open the Basestation. Sign in with your
assigned account. Use Devices to review terminals. Use Instrument Manager to
configure installed instruments and supported outputs.
