# Indi Wi-Fi

Indi Wi-Fi connects industrial sensors and compatible motor or actuator
interfaces to your on-site Modulink Basestation through the site Wi-Fi network.

## Main features

- Two independently wired 4–20 mA input channels.
- One H-bridge interface for compatible motors and actuators.
- 12 VDC or 24 VDC line power.
- Dual-band 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection.

## Use Indi Wi-Fi when

- Equipment is inside reliable site Wi-Fi coverage.
- Your application needs higher data rates.
- You need industrial sensor inputs and a compatible control interface.

## Important limits

The control interface is for non-safety-critical applications. Keep required
safety interlocks independent from Modulink. Confirm the connected equipment is
compatible before you connect it.
