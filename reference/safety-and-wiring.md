# Safety and Field Wiring

Modulink terminals connect to live field equipment. Follow the equipment manual,
site rules, and local electrical rules.

## Before you connect a wire

- Isolate power when the installation procedure requires it.
- Check the equipment manual for voltage, polarity, signal type, and grounding.
- Check that the terminal and the connected equipment are compatible.
- Do not use wire color as the only source of information.

## Analog signals

Wire 4–20 mA and 0–10 V circuits as the instrument manufacturer specifies.
Check if the instrument provides loop power or needs an external supply. Indi
LoRa current inputs do not provide loop power.

## Outputs and actuation

Make the physical system safe before you test a valve, relay, motor interface,
or other output. A status shown in software does not prove the physical equipment
changed state. Use an independent field check when required.

## Support record

When you request support, provide the product name, terminal identity, site,
power source, connected equipment model, and observed condition. Do not send
passwords or device keys in a routine support request.
