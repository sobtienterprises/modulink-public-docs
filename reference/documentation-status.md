## Review rule

Each page has a review date. Check the product label on the equipment before you
use a procedure. Do not use an Indi LoRa (Legacy) procedure for Indi LoRa.

## Public information boundary

Public pages can describe products, installation, and normal operation. They do
not contain passwords, cryptographic keys, factory tests, or internal design
records.

To report a problem, send the page title, product name, terminal identity, and
the observed condition to Modulink support.
